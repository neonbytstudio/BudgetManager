Privacy Policy

Budget Manage & Reminder

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Budget Manage & Reminder (“we,” “our,” or “the App”) collects, uses, and protects information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information you enter stored locally — never transmitted

All financial data you enter — transactions, income, expenses, bills, loans, savings goals, reminders, and categories — is stored only on your device in the App’s local database. This data is never sent to us or to any server. It is not accessible by us, advertisers, or analytics providers.

B. Information collected automatically (via Google services)

The App uses Google services for ads, analytics, and crash reporting, which automatically collect:

Data Purpose Provider
Advertising identifier Show relevant ads and measure ad performance Google AdMob
Device / app-instance IDs Distinct-user analytics Firebase Analytics
Crash logs & diagnostics Fix bugs and improve stability (incl. ANRs) Firebase Crashlytics
App interaction events Understand usage and improve the product Firebase Analytics

2. Advertisements

The App displays ads provided by Google AdMob (banners and interstitial ads). AdMob may use your advertising identifier and device data to serve ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time via your device settings (Google → Ads → “Opt out of Ads Personalization”).

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we request your consent before processing data for ads and analytics. Consent is collected through a consent screen presented on first launch.

3. In-App Purchases (Premium)

Purchasing Premium (removing ads) is processed entirely by Google Play and its billing system. We do not collect or store your payment information. Google Play handles payment details and receipts under its own privacy policy.

4. Permissions We Use

The App requests these permissions, used only for the stated purposes:

Permission Purpose
Camera Scanning paper receipts for expense tracking
Photos / Storage Attaching receipt images to transactions
Notifications Bill and loan payment reminders
Exact alarms Delivery of reminders at precise times

5. How We Protect Your Data

Financial data stays encrypted at rest on your device’s secure app storage. No financial data is transmitted over the internet. Data shared with Google (analytics, crash logs, ads) is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

6. Your Choices

  • Opt out of personalized ads: Device settings → Google → Ads → “Opt out of Ads Personalization”
  • Revoke analytics consent: The EU consent prompt also covers analytics; you may revoke consent at any time in your device’s app settings
  • Delete your data: Uninstalling the App permanently removes all locally stored financial data. Backup files you export are yours and you control them.

7. No Sale of Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. Data shared is limited to the Google services described above for ads, analytics, and crash reporting.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 and over. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Data Retention

Local financial data is retained until you delete it or uninstall the App. Analytics and crash data are retained by Google according to its retention policies. The App has no user accounts, so there is no account data to delete.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the App or legal requirements. Material changes will be posted at this URL with a revised “last updated” date.
