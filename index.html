Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026

Budget Manage & Reminder ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Android application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All data you enter into the app — including transactions, bills, loans, budgets, categories, and payment methods — is stored entirely on your local device.

2. Data Storage

All application data is stored locally in an SQLite database on your device. No data is sent to our servers. You may export and back up your data as a JSON file at any time through the app's settings.

3. Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID) and interaction data to serve ads. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing — Used to process in-app purchases (Remove Ads). Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not have access to your payment details.

4. Permissions

The app requests the following permissions for specific features:

  • Notifications — To send bill and loan payment reminders.
  • Exact Alarms — To schedule reminders at precise times.
  • Boot Completed — To reschedule reminders after your device restarts.
  • Camera — Optional, used for scanning receipts.
  • Storage / Media — To export backup files and import them back.
  • Internet — Required for loading advertisements and currency exchange rates.

5. Data Security

Since all data remains on your device, we do not have access to it. We employ standard Android security practices, including encrypted storage where supported by the operating system.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
